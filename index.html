About me!

Hi! Welcome to my portfolio. It's a combination of academics / career experience and personal projects. For recruiters, if you're looking at this, the 'Engineering' tab is what you're interested in. I am a first-year master's student at Stanford, studying Aeronautics and Astronautics. My focus is to expand my knowledge of jet and rocket propulsion, given my passion for thermal and fluid analysis. I have a passion for making aircraft & rockets more accessible - in terms of sustainability, economics, and production. This involves in particular the design of reusable rockets. This past spring, I graduated from Carnegie Mellon University with my BS in mechanical engineering. As the head mechanic for my buggy racing team, I optimized speed through material selection, improved structural components, and hardware design changes. I gained further experience in design & manufacturing through my role as a technical lead for CMU's team in the NASA Lunabotics competition, where we built a lunar excavation robot. I grew up on the Eastern shore of Maryland and love the outdoors - in my free time I go on runs, and never say no to a hiking opportunity. However I also enjoy art, cooking, baking, and mixology. Not included in my resume are my various roles in the restaurant industry! You can see all my projects under either the art or culinary tabs. I hope you enjoy this sneak peek into my life!
